# Artes v7 — unidade em pixels

Modo: ferramenta de imagem integrada (ImageGen). Fontes preservadas em sources/.
Preparação de cels e terreno em resolução nativa; exportações PNG feitas no Pixelorama 1.2.3 instalado.

Critérios: terreno isométrico 64×32; objetos em pixels de mundo 1:1; sombra e luz da esquerda; paleta compartilhada de 33 cores para arquitetura/terreno; água com oito cels a 8 fps; capa 640×360; retrato 160×192; sem filtro suave. Menus e texto são desenhados pelo jogo.

## props

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production PIXEL ART sprite kit for the isometric action RPG O Nono Véu, to be finished in Pixelorama.
Primary request: twelve coherent ancient Egyptian harbor props. This is authentic low-resolution hand-placed pixel art with large crisp square pixel clusters, not a detailed realistic illustration or 3D render.
Input image: reference of Senu for colors only (teal cape, ivory linen, bronze and dark warm outlines). Do not include characters or reference-board text.
Composition: EXACTLY 4 columns by 3 rows, each object isolated and centered in its own equal cell, substantial transparent gutter on all sides. Isometric 2:1 ground plane, camera sees top and two sides, light from upper left. Same scale language and same palette on every object. No floor patch, no cast shadow, no labels.
Row1: narrow stone obelisk with small bronze sun mark; low brazier on stone plinth with tiny pixel flame; open ruined stone arch; five stone steps.
Row2: wooden merchant canopy with teal-and-ivory striped cloth; single wooden crate with a barrel beside it; date palm; short ruined stone wall.
Row3: sandstone harbor warehouse facade with teal awning; small ancient shrine with two columns; modest wooden boat with single folded sail; short ancient lighthouse.
Style: draw as if each cell is only 96 by 112 pixels, enlarged nearest-neighbor. Visible pixel stair-steps, broad readable shapes and 1 pixel internal accents. 24-32 color restrained palette overall. Deep outlines #182b30, stone #4b5556/#69716c/#96927b, bronze #a47c46/#d3b275, teal #24535a/#3a7170, wood #493d30/#7e6244, ivory #d3c6a0. Flat 3 tone ramps, no black void shading.
Constraints: TRUE TRANSPARENT background, hard alpha edges. No blur, no smooth gradients, no realistic surface noise, no photographic lighting, no bevel-render sheen, no antialiasing, no contact shadow. Each prop complete and disconnected from all neighbors.

## title

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: 16:9 PIXEL ART title screen background, no text, for the video game O Nono Véu.
Primary request: recreate the ancient harbor at sunset and Senu facing it, entirely in authentic deliberate low-resolution pixel art matching a 2D sprite game.
Input image is a reference of actual in-game Senu. Preserve his brown skin, young clean-shaven face, tousled dark hair and ivory linen headband, ONE bronze shoulder guard, cream knee-length linen tunic, worn dark teal cape, leather boots and slim curved khopesh.
Composition: wide panoramic scene, hero on the RIGHT third, seen from behind in 3/4 view, looking over the river toward an ancient sandstone city, obelisks, squat lighthouse, modest sailing boats. LEFT half dark simple readable negative space for the title and buttons which the game draws separately. Strong silhouette, same costume proportions as sprite.
Style: hand-placed pixel clusters as if painted on a 480x270 pixel canvas and enlarged EXACT nearest-neighbor. Obvious square pixels and staircase contours, 32-color palette, 3-4 flat shades per material, selective pixel dithering only on the sky. Stylized environment tiles, not realistic matte painting. Large shapes, sparse details. Skin from amber to umber, teal water and cape, ivory linen, warm ochre sky. Dark teal foreground and distant low-contrast silhouettes.
Lighting: stylized amber sunset with short broken PIXEL reflection bars on water.
Constraints: no text, logo, letters, borders, UI, noise, film grain, painterly gradients, blur, smooth airbrushed shading, photography, 3D render, photorealistic facial detail. Preserve full hero within composition. This should unmistakably belong to a pixel-art RPG.

## portrait

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: pixel-art Senu portrait for the pause menu of O Nono Véu, transparent background.
Input image is the actual in-game Senu reference. Preserve recognizable character and costume: young male brown skin, clean-shaven with dark eyes, tousled black hair and ivory linen headband tied with trailing strips, ONE bronze shoulder guard, simple ivory linen tunic, ragged dark teal scarf and cape. Do not add jewelry or elaborate new armor.
Primary request: waist-up 3/4 portrait looking slightly to the left with calm alert expression. Centered full head and shoulder silhouette, modest heroic proportions. A sprite artist's enlarged character portrait, not realistic concept art.
Style: true deliberate low-resolution PIXEL ART as if on a 160x192 pixel canvas enlarged with nearest neighbor. Hard stepped contours, blocky square pixels, carefully placed 1px facial features, 24-color palette with flat 3-tone ramps. Deep warm outlines, umber skin shadows, ochre highlights, teal cloth, muted bronze, ivory linen. No individual hair strands smaller than the visible pixel grid.
Constraints: TRUE transparent background, hard alpha edges; all hair/headband contained with generous margin, flat bottom cut at waist. No scenery, UI, text, borders, smooth gradients, blur, antialiasing, photographic skin, pores, realistic fabric texture, render lighting or airbrushed shadows. Must match the in-game small pixel sprite's identity.
