# Prompt da prancha de Senu v2

Modo: ferramenta integrada ImageGen. Uso: stylized-concept. Esta prancha é uma referência visual; os sprites jogáveis são produzidos separadamente pelo script Blender do projeto.

Create an original professional game character design sheet for Senu, the young travelling scribe and hermetic disciple from a dark fantasy isometric action RPG set in a fictional Egypt–Axum world. Warm brown skin, short dark hair partly held by a weathered sand-coloured fabric head wrap, an asymmetric worn petroleum-teal cloak and shoulder scarf over layered ivory linen, a single modest worn bronze shoulder guard, leather belt with scribe pouch and rolled parchment, original small geometric gold talisman, practical wrapped travelling boots. An original curved khopesh and two light daggers are shown as equipment studies, not permanently fused to the costume. The character is lean, alert and human, with a readable silhouette, not a bulky medieval knight.

Composition: restrained landscape concept sheet on warm charcoal parchment, one large full-body three-quarter hero illustration, smaller front and back costume studies, material details and a tiny isometric pixel-art translation of the same costume. Maintain one consistent character identity and costume across all views. Elegant painterly art direction, detailed cloth wear, patinated bronze, strong separation of teal cloth, warm skin and sand linen. Dramatic but usable soft light; a sober mysterious atmosphere. No franchise characters, no copied iconography, no Christian/Jewish literal religious symbols, no historical claims, no glossy armour, no giant shoulder pads, no generic hooded assassin, no logo, no watermark, no labels or lettering. The sheet should communicate a travelling scholar who can fight, not an invincible warrior.
